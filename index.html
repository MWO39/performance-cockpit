Roadmap — how to get there

65-week build from your current base · adapts to load & recovery

1
Aerobic base
Wk 1–12 · now
3× Zone 2 per week — build easy runs to 60 min and rides to 2 hr. Keep 2× strength to protect joints and hold muscle. This is the engine everything else sits on.
2
Swim foundation
Wk 13–24
Your weakest discipline, so attack it: 3 swims/week, technique first, then CSS intervals. Goal — 3 km continuous and comfortable by the end of the block.
3
Bike volume
Wk 25–36
Extend the long ride toward 120 km, add one threshold session weekly. Start rehearsing race nutrition on the bike — 60–90 g carbs/hr.
4
Brick & run durability
Wk 37–48
Weekly bike→run bricks to train race legs; long run out to 30 km. Lock fueling and pacing at race intensity — this is where the day is won or lost.
5
Peak & taper
Wk 49–65
One 80% race simulation, then a disciplined 3-week taper. Sharpen, prioritise sleep, run the carb-load protocol — arrive light, fit and fresh.
